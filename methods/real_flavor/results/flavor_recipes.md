# Candidate novel flavor RECIPES (super-chocolate program)

> Flavor RECIPES (blends with proportions), scored by a real pleasantness model (Keller 2016). Odor profiles from Leffingwell descriptors. Every component passes a food-appropriate safety SCREEN (not a GRAS clearance). Proportions are heuristic starting points to be balanced by a flavorist with odor-threshold data. Do NOT ingest without regulatory + safety review.

_Novelty:_ Each recipe is required to be distinct from every reference flavor (chocolate, vanilla, coffee, banana, strawberry, rose) both CHEMICALLY (mean best-match Tanimoto) and PERCEPTUALLY (descriptor-profile cosine), and to share few components with any reference. This demonstrates a flavor character not matching known/public flavors; it does NOT prove absence from proprietary formulas.

## How to make a batch
1. Weigh each aroma component (mg) into a clean amber vial in a fume hood.
2. Add food-grade propylene glycol to the carrier mass shown.
3. Cap, mix to dissolve, equilibrate 24-48 h.
4. Evaluate by SMELL first, far below tasting levels. Do NOT taste without GRAS/FEMA confirmation of every component and use level (see HANDOFF.md).

_Proportions are model-heuristic, not odor-activity-balanced; adjust by nose._
